# KECPA Website — Design System

## Principles
- Financial architecture: disciplined editorial typography and a dimensional growth sculpture.
- The visitor should recognize accounting, tax and advisory immediately. Keep calls to action stationary and preserve native scrolling.
- Illustration represents the idea of growth, not actual client performance or a promised return.

## Foundations
- Ivory #f5f2eb, sand #eae5db, midnight teal/navy #102a34, ink #13272d, copper #b5542e. Warm copper highlights connect all illustrations.
- Playfair Display regular and italic for editorial headings; Inter 400/500/600 for reading and controls. No synthetic bold headings.
- Desktop margins 56px, tablet 32px, mobile 20px/16px. Content capped at 1328px. Sections use 120px, 90px and 70px vertical spacing.
- Breakpoints at 1441, 1100, 900, 760 and 390px. Single-column mobile, opaque mobile menu, two-column desktop.
- Motion: finite column assembly; pointer-responsive sculpture; scroll-driven rotation and column separation; gently folding service panels; process rail and panels; orbit-line rotation in closing statement.
- No text hidden awaiting an observer. No infinite ambient animation. Small screens omit service/process tilting; reduced-motion removes all movement.

## Accessibility
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic HTML, labels, contrast, error messages, and reduced-motion support are required.

## Components
- Buttons: navy/ivory default, copper hover, visible focus outline, disabled waiting state.
- Menu: desktop inline links; mobile closed/open, Escape/outside/focus-leave dismissal, aria-expanded and active link state.
- Form: labelled fields, visible error text with aria-invalid/aria-describedby, loading, success, failure, character count and privacy-storage fallback.
